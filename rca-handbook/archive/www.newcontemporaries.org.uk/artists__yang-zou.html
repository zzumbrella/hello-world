Yang Zou

b. 1987, Shanxi, China

Yang Zou is a moving-image artist. His works, which particularly address post-Soviet, human and machine errors, and the collective anxieties swept up in the current changes of society, combine a purely observational style with poetic elements focusing on people's emotions.

2023-2024, MA Photography, Royal College of Art, London

2006-2011 BA, Photography, Communication University of Zhejiang, Hangzhou

2024–2025
