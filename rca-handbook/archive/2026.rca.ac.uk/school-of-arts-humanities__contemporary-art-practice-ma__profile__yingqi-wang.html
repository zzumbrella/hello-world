About

Yingqi Wang (b. 2001, Canton) is a London-based moving-image artist currently completing an MA in Contemporary Art Practice at the Royal College of Art. Working across experimental video, sound and installation, her practice begins with ordinary actions, familiar objects and unstable materials.
Through repetition, duration and material transformation, she explores tensions between care and damage, control and release, and play and labour. Bodies in her work are often concealed or translated through objects, surfaces and spatial arrangements. Her recent practice has shifted from direct bodily performance towards object-based moving image, using cinematography, editing, sound and looping structures to create sensory environments that encourage slower attention and a temporary withdrawal from productive time.
